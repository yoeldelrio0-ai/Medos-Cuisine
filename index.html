Know Before You Go

What to Expect

🍕
Italian-Inspired Cuisine

Pizza, calzones, stromboli, pasta & more. Our sauce and dough lean slightly sweeter than traditional NY-style — unique and beloved.

🍽️
Very Large Portions

Plan for leftovers. Reviewers consistently rave about the generous sizing — incredible value for your money on every visit.

😊
Warm, Friendly Service

Our team is repeatedly praised for being attentive and welcoming. You'll feel like a regular from your very first visit.

🌿
Vegan & Veggie Options

Multiple meat-free options available across the menu. Welcoming to vegetarian and vegan diners — just ask your server.

⏱️ Heads up on wait times

During peak hours — especially Friday and Saturday evenings — food prep times can run longer than average. Nearly every reviewer agrees: it is absolutely worth the wait. We recommend calling ahead at (434) 836-8355 to place your order early.
