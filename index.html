Know Before You Go

What to Expect

Everything you need for the best experience at New York Pizzeria.

🍕
Italian-Inspired Cuisine
Pizza, calzones, strombolis, pasta, wings, gyros & more. Our sauce and dough lean slightly sweeter than traditional NY-style.
🍽️
Very Large Portions
Plan to take leftovers! Reviewers consistently mention the generous sizing — incredible value for money.
😊
Friendly, Warm Service
Our team is routinely praised for being welcoming and attentive. Expect to be treated like family every visit.
🌿
Vegetarian & Vegan Options
Multiple meat-free options available. (Not currently known for gluten-free options.)
👶
Kids' Menu Available
Family-friendly with a kids' menu, outdoor seating, and a welcoming atmosphere for all ages.

Service Options

How We Work

🏠
Dine-In & Outdoor Seating
Enjoy our cozy indoor dining room or our outdoor seating area. Great for families, dates, and casual lunches.
📦
Takeout — Call Ahead
Call (434) 836-8355 to place your order for pickup. No drive-through, but walk-in pickup is available.
🛵
Delivery Available
Standard delivery service to your door. Note: no-contact delivery is not currently offered.
📅
Closed Sundays
We're open Monday–Saturday. Closed on Sundays — plan your visit accordingly.
⏱️ Heads Up on Wait Times
During peak hours, wait times can be longer — but nearly every reviewer agrees it's worth the wait. We recommend calling ahead, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings.
