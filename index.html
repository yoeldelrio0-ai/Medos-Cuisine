Know Before You Go

What to Expect

Everything you need to know to have the best experience at New York Pizzeria.

🍕
Italian-Style Cuisine
Pizza, calzones, and strombolis are our specialties — crafted with a unique homestyle touch. Note: our sauce and dough lean slightly sweeter than classic NY-style.
🍽️
Very Large Portions
Plan to take leftovers! Customers consistently comment that the portions are generously sized and well worth the price.
😊
Friendly, Attentive Service
Our team is routinely praised for being warm, welcoming, and outstanding. Expect to be treated like family from the moment you walk in.
🌿
Vegetarian & Vegan Options
Multiple meat-free options available. We cater to vegetarian and vegan diners with dedicated menu items. (Not currently known for gluten-free options.)

Service Options

How We Work

🏠
Dine-In Available
Enjoy our cozy, casual dining room. Great for date nights, family dinners, and casual lunches.
📦
Takeout Available
Call ahead for your order and pick it up fresh and ready. No drive-through, but walk-in pickup is quick and easy.
🛵
Delivery Available
Get your favorite dishes delivered. Note: no-contact delivery is not currently available, but standard delivery service is offered.
⏱️ Heads Up on Wait Times
During peak hours, food prep times can be longer than average — but nearly every reviewer agrees it's 100% worth the wait. We recommend calling ahead or arriving early on weekends.
